About

A fan night market for the wait.

Leonida Drop is an unofficial companion for people counting down to Grand Theft Auto VI (November 19, 2026, PS5 and Xbox Series). Story pages use only public Rockstar / Take-Two facts. Shop items are original art in a Vice City night palette — not Rockstar assets, not in-game items, not the official Ultimate Edition.

How a buy works

  1. Connect MetaMask, Coinbase Wallet, Trust, Rainbow, OKX (Polygon) or Phantom / Solflare (Solana). On phone, the button opens the app.
  2. You send the USD price as USDT/POL on Polygon, or SOL on Solana, to the published treasury.
  3. That transaction is the on-chain proof.
  4. We mint a locker NFT record to the paying address: unique token id, item, rarity, tx hash.

Polygon is used so a $4 tank does not die in gas. If you have USDT on Polygon, we spend that first. If not, native POL. Phantom sends SOL.
