Official, as of August 2026

Vice City, USA. The state of Leonida.

Everything here is what Rockstar has put on rockstargames.com/VI, trailers, and press. No leaks dressed up as facts.

Release November 19, 2026 · PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S. Trailer 1 promised 2025, then May 26 2026, then this date. PC has not been announced.
Story “Jason and Lucia have always known the deck is stacked against them.” An easy score goes wrong. They are pulled into a criminal conspiracy across Leonida and have to rely on each other to get out alive.
Lucia Caminos First female protagonist in a numbered GTA. Her father taught her to fight. Fighting for her family put her in Leonida Penitentiary. Sheer luck got her out. Smart moves only, from here.
Jason Duval Grew up around grifters. Army, then the Keys, working for local drug runners. Wants an easy life. Does not get one. Lives on property tied to Brian Heder.
The map Vice City (Miami) plus Leonida Keys, Grassrivers, Port Gellhorn, Ambrosia, Mount Kalaga National Park. Vice City is described as twice Los Santos. Rockstar filmed and researched on the ground in Florida.
What else is confirmed Dual protagonists you can play as a couple or more like partners. Trailers 1 and 2. Netflix “An Extended Look” (in-game PS5 footage, ~27 minutes, Aug 27 2026). Pre-orders live. Ultimate Edition and a Vintage Vice City Pack exist as official pre-order items — not sold here.

People around them

“A life with Jason could be her way out.”

Rockstar has named supporting pieces: Cal Hampton (Jason’s conspiracy-minded friend), Boobie Ike (Vice City operator), Dre’Quan Priest and Real Dimez, bank robber Raul Bautista, Brian and Lori Heder, Stephanie on Lucia’s paperwork. The rest of the cast lives in the trailers: spring break, swamp mystics, strip-club economy, a state that satirizes Florida without being Florida.
